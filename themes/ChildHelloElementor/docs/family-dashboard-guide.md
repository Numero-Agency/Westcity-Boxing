# West City Boxing - Family Dashboard Guide

## What is the Family Dashboard?

The Family Dashboard is a convenient tool that allows parents to manage all their children's West City Boxing memberships from one place. Instead of logging into multiple accounts, you can view and manage memberships, renewals, and payments for all your children in a single dashboard.

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Log In

1. Go to **westcityboxing.nz/family-login**
2. Use **any of your children's account credentials** to log in:
   - **Email:** The email address you used when registering your child
   - **Password:** The password you created during registration
3. You'll be automatically redirected to the Family Dashboard

> **Note:** You can use whichever child's account is easiest for you to remember. All your children can be linked to this dashboard.

### Step 2: Link Your Children's Accounts

Once logged in, you'll see the Family Dashboard. If this is your first time:

1. Scroll down to the **"Link Your Child's Account"** section
2. Enter your child's **email address** or **name**
3. Click **"Link Child Account"**
4. Repeat for each child you want to add

Your children will now appear in the dashboard, and you can manage all their memberships from here.

---

## Understanding the Dashboard

### Membership Cards

Each child appears as a card showing:

- **Child's Name** - The member's display name
- **Program** - Which program they're enrolled in (e.g., "Mini Cadet Boys (9-11 Years) Group 1")
- **Status Badge** - Current membership status (see below)
- **Action Buttons** - Options to manage their membership

### Membership Status Types

| Status | What It Means | Action |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| **Active** | Membership is current and valid | No action needed |
| **Active Subscription** | Online recurring payment is set up and active | Manage Subscription |
| **Expires Soon** | Membership will expire within 7 days | Manage Subscription |
| **Expired** | Online payment subscription has ended | Manage Subscription to renew |
| **Paused** | Subscription is temporarily paused | Manage Subscription to resume |
| **On Waitlist** | Child is on the waitlist for a program | Choose Payment Plan when spot available |
| **Needs Activation** | No online payment set up, or manual payment period ended | Choose Payment Plan |

## Managing Memberships

### For Active Subscriptions (Online Payment)

If you have set up online payments through Stripe, click **"Manage Subscription"** to:

- **View subscription details** - See billing amount, frequency, and next payment date
- **View recent payments** - See your payment history
- **Change billing cycle** - Switch between weekly, fortnightly, or other payment options
- **Pause subscription** - Temporarily pause payments (can be resumed anytime)
- **Cancel subscription** - End the recurring payments
- **Access Stripe Portal** - Manage payment methods directly with Stripe

### For Paused Subscriptions

Click **"Manage Subscription"** to:

- **Resume subscription** - Restart your paused subscription
- **Cancel subscription** - End the subscription entirely
- **Access Stripe Portal** - Update payment details

### For Expired Memberships (Online Payment)

If you previously had online payments set up through Stripe, click **"Manage Subscription"** to:

- View your previous subscription details
- Set up a new payment plan to renew
- Access billing options for your child's program

### For Members Needing Activation

This status appears when:
- Your child was registered but hasn't set up online payments yet
- A previous manual/cash payment period has ended

Click **"Choose Payment Plan"** to select and activate an online payment plan for the membership.

### For Waitlist Members

Click **"Choose Payment Plan"** to view available membership options when a spot becomes available in your child's program.

## Viewing Member Details

Click **"View Details"** on any child's card to see:

- Full member information
- Current subscription details
- Payment history
- Membership start and due dates

## Removing a Child from Your Dashboard

If you need to remove a child from your Family Dashboard:

1. Click the **"Remove"** button on their card
2. Confirm the removal

> **Important:** This only removes them from YOUR dashboard view. Their membership remains active and unaffected.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use any of my children's accounts to log in?

Yes! You can log in using any of your children's account credentials. Once logged in, you can link all your other children to manage everyone from one place.

### What if I forget my password?

Click **"Forgot your password?"** on the login page. A password reset link will be sent to the account's email address.

### Will my child still be able to log into their own account?

Yes, your children can still log into their individual accounts. The Family Dashboard is an additional way for parents to manage memberships - it doesn't replace individual account access.

### Can both parents access the Family Dashboard?

Yes, both parents can access the dashboard by logging in with any linked child's account credentials.

### What happens when I pause a subscription?

- Billing is temporarily stopped
- The membership remains valid until the current paid period ends
- You can resume the subscription at any time from the dashboard
- When resumed, billing continues from the next billing cycle

### How do I add a new child who just joined?

1. Make sure the new child has been registered with West City Boxing and has an account
2. Go to your Family Dashboard
3. Use the **"Link Your Child's Account"** section
4. Enter their email or username and click "Link Child Account"

### What if I can't link my child's account?

The child must have a membership record in our system to be linked. If you're having trouble:
- Make sure you're using the correct email or username
- Contact West City Boxing if you need assistance
